Bespoke Perfumery & Designer Replicas

How We Replicate Luxury Scents

We master the art of molecular perfumery. By deconstructing the exact notes of iconic designer masterpieces—such as Dior Sauvage, Gucci Flora, Creed Aventus, and Blue de Chanel—we formulate exceptional, long-lasting replicas that deliver the identical luxury olfactory experience at an accessible price.

1. Molecular Deconstruction

Using advanced gas chromatography, we deconstruct the top, heart, and base notes of original international luxury perfumes to match the exact scent profile.

2. French Oils & Longevity

We blend high-concentration French fragrance oils (30%+ Extrait de Parfum) to replicate the designer scent while ensuring superior longevity in the Indian climate.

3. Custom Fragrance Matching

Want a custom replica? Simply tell us the designer brand name or list your desired olfactory notes, and our perfumers will craft a signature bottle exclusively for you.

Replicate Any Designer Scent

Can't find your favorite luxury perfume? We can formulate exact copies of any premium brand scent—from Gucci, Dior, Chanel, and Creed, to Tom Ford and Armani. Start your custom request today.
